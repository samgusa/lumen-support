# Privacy Policy for Lumen Last Updated: May 2026 Lumen respects your privacy. ## Information We Collect Lumen does not require user accounts. Lumen does not collect personal information such as names, email addresses, phone numbers, or location data. Lumen does not use advertising identifiers. Lumen does not track users across apps or websites. ## Video Content Lumen allows users to import and manage their own video files. Imported videos may be stored locally on the user's device or referenced from user-selected file locations, depending on the feature being used. Video files remain under the user's control and are not uploaded to servers operated by the developer. ## Third-Party Storage Providers Users may choose to access files stored in third-party services such as cloud storage providers through Apple's Files integration. Any interaction with third-party storage providers is initiated and controlled by the user. Lumen does not automatically upload user content to third-party services. ## Purchases Lumen offers optional premium features through Apple's In-App Purchase system. Purchase transactions are processed by Apple. The developer does not receive payment card information. ## Analytics and Tracking Lumen does not use analytics services. Lumen does not use advertising services. Lumen does not track users. ## Data Security Because Lumen does not collect or store personal user information on developer-operated servers, user content remains on storage locations chosen by the user. ## Children's Privacy Lumen does not knowingly collect personal information from children. ## Contact For privacy-related questions, contact: [greenhillsam25@gmail.com](mailto:greenhillsam25@gmail.com) ## Changes to This Policy This Privacy Policy may be updated from time to time.
